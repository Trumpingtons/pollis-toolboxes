## How the Market Values a Company

Investors value a bicycle maker at about eight years of its yearly earnings, plus its cash, less its debt. Savings a deal is still to bring count as earnings from the day it is signed. The share price is that value divided by the number of shares.

A bid moves the price of the company it is made for. When the highest bid a company received is above its value per share and the company is not sold, its price moves halfway from its value toward that bid: investors expect another bid to come.

## Earnings

Each quarter a company's earnings grow, Orvane Wheelworks' fastest and Larkspur Cycles' slowest, and a random shock moves them up or down by a few percent.

## The Walk-Away Price

Your walk-away price protects your shareholders. Set it at the price your company is worth to them on its own, plus the share of the savings a buyer should pay them to sell. A walk-away price below your share price lets a rival buy your company for less than the market thinks it is worth.
