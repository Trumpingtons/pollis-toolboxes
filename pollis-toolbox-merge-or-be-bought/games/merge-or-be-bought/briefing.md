# Merge or Be Bought

Three bicycle makers are listed on the stock exchange: Larkspur Cycles, the largest, Tamberlin Bikes and Orvane Wheelworks, the smallest and fastest growing. Your team is the board of one of them. Each board can try to buy a rival, and each can be bought.

## The Game

- The game lasts six quarters. Each quarter your board sets a bid per share for each rival still listed (0 makes no bid) and its walk-away price: the lowest bid for your own shares that your board accepts.
- A company receives the highest bid made for it. When that bid is at least its walk-away price, the company is bought: its shareholders are paid the bid in cash, and the buyer takes on its business, its cash and its debt.
- A company that is bought leaves the game, and its shareholders keep the price they were paid. Its board watches the rest of the game.
- After each quarter everyone sees each company's share price, the highest bid it received, its market value and earnings, and every bid made. Only your board sees its own accounts: cash, debt, what it can still borrow, and the most it can pay.
- The winner is the company whose original shareholders have the best return: the share price after the sixth quarter, or the price the company was bought at, over its price before the first quarter.
- Your board can write to the other boards, beside the chat. The facilitator reads those messages too, and nothing makes a board keep what it promised.
- During the game the facilitator releases news, which can change interest rates, growth or what investors pay for earnings. Some news reaches only some roles.

## What Only Your Board Knows

Before the first quarter your analysts give your board their estimate of the yearly savings of buying each rival: shared factories, shops and suppliers. The other boards have their own estimates, which you do not see.

## Your Briefing

Your role's briefing, below this one, says how the market values a company, how bids and deals work, and how a bid is paid for. If your board has several roles, each knows something the others do not: talk before anyone submits.
