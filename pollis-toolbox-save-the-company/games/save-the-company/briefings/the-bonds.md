## The Bondholders' Choices

Each quarter you set the largest cut to your bonds you accept, and the least share of the company you want for it. A cut of 100% with a large share of the company swaps your bonds for ownership.

You can also sell your bonds. Selling ends your part in the game at the buyer's price, which is then your result, and the buyer negotiates in your place. Your accounts show what the buyer would pay.
