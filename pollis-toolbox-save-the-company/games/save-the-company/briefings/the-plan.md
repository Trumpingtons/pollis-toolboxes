## Proposing a Plan

A plan is put to the lenders only when you choose Propose This Plan. It sets:

- **New money:** cash the shareholders put in. A plan must leave Thistledown at least 20 m £ of cash.
- **The cut to the banks' loans** and **the cut to the bonds**, as shares of what is owed.
- **The share of the company for the bondholders:** the shares they receive for their cut.

The banks agree when their cut is no larger than the largest they accept; the bondholders when their cut is no larger than theirs and their share of the company no smaller than the least they accept. Neither side tells you its terms unless it wants to: ask in Negotiations.

Every quarter without a plan burns cash and lets earnings fall further. The banks can call in the loan at any time.
