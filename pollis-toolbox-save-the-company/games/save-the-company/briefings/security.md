## The Banks' Choices

Each quarter you set the largest cut to your loans you accept in a plan. Accept too little and the plan fails; accept too much and you give away value the security would have kept.

Calling in the loan puts Thistledown into administration this quarter: its stores and stock are sold, the administrators take their costs, and you are paid first from what is left. Your accounts show what you would recover if the company were wound up now, by your appraiser's estimate.
