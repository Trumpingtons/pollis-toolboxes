## Running the Stores

Each quarter the Chief Executive can close 20 loss-making stores, up to 60 in all. Closing them costs cash now, and adds to every later quarter's earnings, which raises what the company is worth under a plan.

Each quarter Thistledown pays interest on its loans and bonds and spends about 1.5 m £ on its stores. When that is more than its earnings, the cash falls. Your accounts show the cash and how much the next quarter will burn.
