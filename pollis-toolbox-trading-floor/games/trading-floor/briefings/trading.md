## Placing an Order

An order buys or sells a number of shares, up to 100, at a price or better: a buy order at that price or lower, a sell order at that price or higher. Prices go in steps of £0.05.

- An order that can trade at once does: with the best prices on the other side of the book first and, at the same price, the oldest order first. It trades at the price of the order already on the book.
- What does not trade stays on the book until another desk trades with it, someone in your desk cancels it, or the period closes. Orders still on the book at the close are cancelled.
- Your desk can buy only with the cash its other buy orders do not hold, and sell only the shares its other sell orders do not hold: no borrowing, and no selling shares it does not have.
- An order that would trade with your own desk's order is refused: cancel your own order first.

## Reading the Book

The order book shows the best prices to sell above and the best prices to buy below, with the shares at each, and the last trades. When the best price to buy is close to the best price to sell, the market agrees on what the share is worth; a desk with a hint can trade before the others learn what it knows.
