# Trading Floor

Your team is a trading desk on the market for one share: Corvane Robotics, a listed maker of warehouse robots. The other teams are desks too, and the same order book serves you all.

## The Game

- The game lasts four or six periods, as the facilitator chose. Each period trades for a few minutes by the clock, beside the chat.
- Every desk starts with £5,000 of cash and 100 shares. Corvane's shares are valued at £50.00 to begin with.
- At the close of each period, Corvane announces its results, which move the value of its shares up or down, usually by less than £8 a share.
- Before each period, every other desk, in turn, receives its analysts' hint of the move the results will bring, usually right to within about £2. The other desks do not know which desks have a hint, or what it says.
- During a period the facilitator can release news, which everyone reads at once. It moves the value at that period's close.
- At the end, Corvane is taken over at its value: every desk's shares are paid that value in cash.
- The desk with the best return wins: its cash and its shares at Corvane's final value, over its £10,000 at the start.
- Desks can write to each other in Negotiations, beside the chat. The facilitator reads those messages too, and nothing makes a desk keep what it promised, or tell the truth.

## The Results

After each period everyone sees Corvane's results and new value, how many shares traded and at what price, and each desk's cash, shares and wealth at that value. Only your desk sees what it bought and sold, and at what average prices.
