# Archaeology Toolbox

Version 0.1.1

**Archaeology: Prospection and Ancient Places.** Earthworks found in lidar terrain models and sites in satellite images, with the trained models of the literature applied in Julia, and the ancient places of registers not bound to one country. For archaeologists, heritage officers and museums.

Installing it adds the **Archaeology** submenu to the end of the **Extend** menu; its **Edit** button in the Installed section of the Extensions pane moves it, or any of its panels, to another menu. Each panel has key points, a decision table, example code you can send to the Julia REPL or a notebook, reference wikis and Next Steps.

The menu has the submenu Archaeological Prospection, which holds Terrain Model from Point Cloud, Relief Visualisation, Candidate Features, Learned Detection in Lidar, Learned Detection in Satellite Images and Enki on Held-Out Ground; then Sites and Monuments. One menu item per panel, which opens on its first tab, or at its first step for Terrain Model from Point Cloud, Learned Detection in Lidar and Learned Detection in Satellite Images, whose panels have numbered steps.

## Requirements

Julia. Each panel lists the Julia packages it uses on its Powered by line and offers to install the missing ones. The panels read and write their files of places and features through DuckDB's spatial extension, which Pollis downloads from DuckDB's repository when the toolbox is installed, and keeps if the toolbox is uninstalled. The panels of Archaeological Prospection need the Internet for their example terrain model, which they keep in their Folder, and for the imagery beside their maps. Sites and Monuments needs it to download Pleiades and the Ancient Near East site placemarks once into its Folder and for its maps: Google's maps need a Google Maps key and Esri World Imagery an ArcGIS key, both kept by its Credentials line, while its free map needs none.
