# Administrative and Statistical Units

## Administrative Units in INSPIRE

Administrative units are a theme of Annex I of the INSPIRE Directive ([European Parliament and Council of the European Union (2007)](#ref-EPCEU2007a)). Regulation (EU) No 1089/2010 defines the administrative unit as a unit of administration where a member state has and/or exercises jurisdictional rights, for local, regional and national governance ([European Commission (2010)](#ref-EC2010a)). The theme's data specification adds the administrative boundary, the line between units, and the condominium, an area administered by two or more countries together ([INSPIRE Maintenance and Implementation Group (2024)](#ref-IMIG2024a)). The Theme input of Find Datasets in the INSPIRE Data panel searches the datasets of administrative units with Administrative Units.

## The Levels of Administrative Units

The administrative units of a country form a hierarchy: each unit has its level, from the country itself down to the smallest units, such as municipalities, and the national name of that level, and a unit links to the unit one level up ([INSPIRE Maintenance and Implementation Group (2024)](#ref-IMIG2024a)). The levels of one country need not correspond to those of another. Units by Level in the INSPIRE Data panel counts the units of each value of Colour By, with their total, median and largest areas: the levels that cover the whole country have the same total area.

## Statistical Units in INSPIRE

Statistical units are a theme of Annex III of the INSPIRE Directive ([European Parliament and Council of the European Union (2007)](#ref-EPCEU2007a)): the units for the dissemination or use of statistical information, as vector units, such as census districts or the NUTS regions, or as the cells of statistical grids ([INSPIRE Maintenance and Implementation Group (2024)](#ref-IMIG2024b)). The Theme input of Find Datasets in the INSPIRE Data panel searches the datasets of statistical units with Statistical Units; their vector units are read like administrative units, with the Name Column and Colour By set to the columns of their service.

## A Country's Units Beside GISCO's

GISCO distributes the local administrative units of the whole of Europe, the municipalities and communes on which Eurostat builds the NUTS regions, in one file for each year ([Eurostat (2026)](#ref-Eur2026a)). Beside GISCO's Units in the INSPIRE Data panel takes the level with the most units in the dataset read, its value of Colour By found most often, and sets it beside GISCO's local administrative units of 2024 for the Country: the number of units of each, their total areas, the names found in both, the units of the same name whose areas differ most, and the names found in one only. The two may differ in their date, their scale and the areas they include, such as inland and coastal waters.
