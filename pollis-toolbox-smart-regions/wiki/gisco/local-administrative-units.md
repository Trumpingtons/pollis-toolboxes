# Local Administrative Units

## What a LAU Is

Local administrative units (LAUs) are the municipalities and communes of the European Union, in a system that Eurostat maintains compatible with NUTS to meet the demand for statistics at a local level: they are the building blocks of NUTS ([Eurostat (2026)](#ref-Eur2026c)). LAUs are administrative, so that data are readily available and policies can be implemented with them; they subdivide the NUTS 3 regions and cover the EU's whole economic territory.

## One Level Since 2017

There used to be two LAU levels: an upper level, LAU 1, formerly NUTS level 4, defined for most but not all countries, and a lower level, LAU 2, formerly NUTS level 5, of municipalities or equivalent units ([Eurostat (2026)](#ref-Eur2026c)). Since 2017 there has been only one LAU level.

## Yearly Lists

LAUs change frequently, so Eurostat publishes an updated list, a correspondence table between LAUs and NUTS 3 regions, towards the end of each year ([Eurostat (2026)](#ref-Eur2026c)). EU countries send their lists of LAUs to Eurostat once a year, with each LAU's total population and total area where available. GISCO distributes the LAU boundaries of each year from 2011, in one file for all countries at 1:1 million ([Eurostat (2026)](#ref-Eur2026a)).

## LAUs, Typologies and Cities

The LAUs serve to apply the local typologies of Tercet: the coastal areas and the degree of urbanisation (DEGURBA), with the definitions of cities and functional urban areas ([Eurostat (2026)](#ref-Eur2026c); [Eurostat (2019)](#ref-Eur2019a)). Eurostat lists the LAUs of which each city and each functional urban area is composed. A functional urban area is a city with its commuting zone ([Dijkstra, Poelman and Veneri (2019)](#ref-DPV2019a)).

## LAUs in the Eurostat GISCO Panel

The Local Administrative Units tab of the Eurostat GISCO panel downloads GISCO's LAU file of the Year chosen once, to the Folder, and reads the units of the Country chosen, with their code GISCO_ID, their name, and, in recent files, their population, density and area ([Eurostat (2026)](#ref-Eur2026a)). Population and Density sums the population of the units and lists the most populous and the densest; Countries Side by Side compares the number of LAUs, their median area and population, and the share of small units, across all the countries of the file.

## LAUs of Different Sizes

The number and size of LAUs differ between countries, as Countries Side by Side in the Eurostat GISCO panel shows. Analyses of data from areal units depend on the scale and zoning of the units, the modifiable areal unit problem, which Fotheringham and Wong found to be unpredictable in its intensity and effects in multivariate analysis ([Fotheringham and Wong (1991)](#ref-FW1991a)).
