# Self-Study: Learn and Practise

Turn your slides, notes and PDFs into what helps you study: more problems with their solutions, a topic explained in depth, a summary of a paper, prose and audio from slides, or slides from a long text. For students, and for teachers preparing their classes.

Installing it adds the **Self-Study: Learn and Practise** submenu to the end of the **Extend** menu; its **Edit** button in the Installed section of the Extensions pane moves it, or any of its panels, to another menu. Each panel has key points, a decision table, example code you can send to the Julia REPL or a notebook, reference wikis and notebook tutorials.

## Panels

| Panel | What it covers | Powered by |
|---|---|---|
| Practice | Multiple-choice exercises on descriptive statistics, hypothesis tests and linear regression, new data for each seed, with checked answers and explanations | [Distributions.jl](https://github.com/JuliaStats/Distributions.jl) |
| Write an Exercise | A template for new exercises, computed from data or conceptual, with previews of their versions and a check that no two choices coincide | [Distributions.jl](https://github.com/JuliaStats/Distributions.jl) |

## Data-Driven Decisions: Computational Lab Series

A submenu of graduate lab courses, each a page of numbered steps, one a lab, with the datasets they use in Manage Competition Datasets.

| Lab course | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Forecasting: From Univariate Parsimony to Probabilistic Ensembles | Three labs, from univariate parsimony on Tourism (2011), to global cross-learning and multiple seasonalities on NN5, and probabilistic covariate modeling on GEFCom 2014 |
| Financial Volatility & Return Predictability: From Parametric GARCH to High-Frequency Limit Order Books | Four labs, from daily volatility and tail risk on the Oxford-Man Realized Library, to intraday volatility on Optiver, cross-sectional asset pricing on OSAP and Gu, Kelly and Xiu (2020), and limit order book dynamics on FI-2010 |
| Enterprise Valuation: From Discounted Cash Flows to Stochastic Simulation | Four labs, from WACC, APV and Capital Cash Flow equivalence, to cross-sectional multiples, stochastic cash flows with tax shields and distress, and real options with structural credit pricing |
| Causal Inference & Policy Evaluation: From Natural Experiments to Double Machine Learning | Four labs, from LaLonde's NSW benchmark, to Card and Krueger and Proposition 99, Double Machine Learning on 401(k) eligibility, and uplift and policy learning on Hillstrom and Criteo |
| Operations, Pricing & Supply Chain Optimization: From Classical Inventory Rules to Decision-Focused Learning | Four labs, from censored newsvendor policies on VN2 and Favorita, to vehicle routing on Amazon Last-Mile, dynamic pricing with contextual bandits, and decision-focused learning on ICON scheduling |

## Requirements

Pollis and Julia. Each panel lists the Julia packages it uses on its Powered by line and offers to install the missing ones.
