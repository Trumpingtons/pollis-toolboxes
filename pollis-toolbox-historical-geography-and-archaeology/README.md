# Historical Geography and Archaeology Toolbox

**Historical Geography and Archaeology: The Past on the Map.** The borders, places and landscapes of the past, read from open data and mapped in Julia. For historians, archaeologists, heritage officers and museums, and for the municipalities and regional authorities whose places have history attached.

Installing it adds the **Historical Geography and Archaeology** submenu to the end of the **Extend** menu; its **Edit** button in the Installed section of the Extensions pane moves it, or any of its panels, to another menu. Each panel has key points, a decision table, example code you can send to the Julia REPL or a notebook, reference wikis and Next Steps.

The menu has Historical Borders, then, greyed, Old Maps, Historical Populations and Historical Episodes; then the submenu Archaeological Prospection, which holds Relief Visualisation, Candidate Features, Learned Detection in Lidar and Learned Detection in Satellite Images; then, greyed, Sites and Monuments and Landscape Analysis. One menu item per panel, which opens on its first tab, or at its first step for Learned Detection in Lidar and Learned Detection in Satellite Images, whose panels have numbered steps.

## Requirements

Julia. Each panel lists the Julia packages it uses on its Powered by line and offers to install the missing ones. Historical Borders reads geographic files through DuckDB's spatial extension, which Pollis downloads from DuckDB's repository when the toolbox is installed, and keeps if the toolbox is uninstalled. Historical Borders needs the Internet to download each source once into its Folder, and for the base map under the slider. The panels of Archaeological Prospection need it for their example terrain model, which they keep in their Folder, and for the imagery beside their maps.

The data belong to their authors, whose terms apply: [Cliopatria](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria), [historical-basemaps](https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps) and [CShapes 2.0](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/cshapes/).
