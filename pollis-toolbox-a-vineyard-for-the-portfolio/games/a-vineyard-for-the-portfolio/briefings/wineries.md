## Your Winery

Your family owns one of the three wineries. Before Due Diligence, the model tells your team something about the winery that only you know, and that Harrowgate's diligence may find.

Selling to Harrowgate turns the winery into cash and an earn-out. Keeping it leaves you with its earnings and its value at the end of Year 3, whatever they turn out to be. Either way your team runs the winery in Years 1 to 3.
