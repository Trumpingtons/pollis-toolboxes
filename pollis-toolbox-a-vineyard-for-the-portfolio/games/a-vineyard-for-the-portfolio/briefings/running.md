## Running the Winery

Each year from Year 1 your team sets:

- **Prices:** raised, each bottle earns about 5% more and sales grow about 3 points a year less; cut, the opposite.
- **Marketing:** spent this year; it adds to this year's growth, less for each further pound.
- **Investment:** spent on vines and cellars this year; it adds about 0.4 points to every later year's growth for each m £.

Your earnings in pounds also move with your currency against the pound, with your harvest, and with your export markets. A winery Harrowgate owns pays its marketing and investment from Harrowgate's pocket, and Harrowgate's distribution can add to its growth.
