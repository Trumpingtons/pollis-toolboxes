## Selling the Winery

In Due Diligence, your owners decide whether to disclose your winery's issue to Harrowgate. Harrowgate's diligence may find it anyway.

In First Offers and Last Offers, your owners set the least cash they accept, and the least cash and earn-out, the earn-out counted in full. When Harrowgate's offer meets both, the deal is signed: you are paid the cash, and the earn-out after Year 3, in the share of the plan's earnings the winery reaches in Year 3. Harrowgate offers for one winery a round, and you see its offer only when it is for yours.

Your owners' score counts the winery at its stated value to begin with. Sold, they have what Harrowgate pays; kept, they have the winery's earnings and its value at the end of Year 3. Your issue does not go away when the winery is sold: it lowers the earn-out instead.
