## Currencies

Each winery earns in its own currency: the Chilean peso, the euro or the Australian dollar. Harrowgate reports in pounds, so every move of those currencies against the pound moves the winery's earnings in pounds, by about 5% a year at random, and more when the news brings a shock.

## The Hedge

For the winery Harrowgate owns, Treasury chooses each year the share of its earnings whose exchange rate is fixed, from 0% to 100%. The hedged share is kept from the currency's move, up or down, and hedging costs about 1% of the earnings hedged. The winery's own earnings, and the earn-out, still move with the currency: the hedge protects Harrowgate alone.
