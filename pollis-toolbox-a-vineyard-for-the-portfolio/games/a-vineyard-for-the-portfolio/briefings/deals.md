## Due Diligence

For each winery, Harrowgate can do no diligence, a desk review (0.2 m £), which finds a problem roughly, or full due diligence (0.6 m £), which finds it as it is. What the owners disclose, you learn for nothing.

## Offers

In First Offers and Last Offers, Harrowgate offers for one winery: cash, paid when the deal is signed, and an earn-out, paid after Year 3. The earn-out pays the share of its plan's earnings the winery reaches in Year 3, at most all of it; the plan is its stated earnings grown at its stated growth. An earn-out shifts the risk of a winery that is not what its owners say onto its owners.

The owners set the least cash, and the least cash and earn-out (counted in full), they accept, and see your offer whether or not it meets them. Once a deal is signed in First Offers, Last Offers counts for nothing. Use Negotiations to learn what the owners want before you offer.
