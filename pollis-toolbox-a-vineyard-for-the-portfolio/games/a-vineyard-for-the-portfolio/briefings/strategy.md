## Choosing a Winery

The three wineries differ in size, growth and fit:

- Viña Piedra Azul grows fastest, and Chilean wine sells well at moderate prices in Harrowgate's markets.
- Tenuta Ventaluce is the smallest, and Tuscan wine fits Harrowgate's premium brands best: its distribution adds the most growth there.
- Kestrel Flats Estate earns the most, and Australian wine sells mostly in Asia, where Harrowgate is weakest: its distribution adds the least growth there.

## Distribution

Once Harrowgate owns a winery, its distribution network can sell the winery's wine: in some markets, for about 0.3 m £ a year, or in every market, for about 0.6 m £ a year. Distribution raises the winery's yearly growth, by more where the wine fits Harrowgate's markets: up to about 8 points a year for Tenuta Ventaluce, 5 for Viña Piedra Azul and 4 for Kestrel Flats Estate, half as much in some markets.
