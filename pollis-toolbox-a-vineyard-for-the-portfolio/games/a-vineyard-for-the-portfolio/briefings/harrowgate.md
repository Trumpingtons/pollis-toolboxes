## Harrowgate's Position

Harrowgate's analysts have estimated the savings of owning each winery: bottling, buying and overheads shared with the group. Only your board knows these estimates, which arrive before Due Diligence.

Harrowgate can buy one winery. Every pound it pays above the winery's value to Harrowgate, its true earnings with the savings, valued at the market's multiple, is value lost. Every pound below is value created.

The wineries' owners know their wineries better than you do. What they do not tell you, your diligence may find.
