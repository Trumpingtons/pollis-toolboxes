# Costs and Cash

- **Each tablet sold** costs its beans, which Sourcing knows, and 0.40 € for everything else: milk, sugar, wrapping and transport.
- **Each shop** costs 40,000 € of rent a year, and 80,000 € to open, once, in the year it opens. Closing a shop costs nothing.
- **Each member of staff** costs 22,000 € a year.
- **Marketing** is spent in full in the year it is spent.
- **Profit** is the revenue less the cost of the tablets, the rent, the wages, the shops opened and the marketing. Cash is the 1,000,000 € you start with plus every year's profit. Cash can fall below zero; the winner is the team with the highest cumulative profit.
- **An example.** A shop with 3 staff costs 106,000 € a year in rent and wages. Selling at 4 € with beans that cost 1 € a tablet, each tablet leaves 2.60 €, so the shop covers its rent and wages from about 41,000 tablets a year.
