# Shops and Staff

- **Capacity.** Each member of staff sells up to 20,000 tablets a year. A brand can sell at most its shops times its staff per shop times 20,000 tablets; buyers it cannot serve are lost, and show as Lost Sales in your accounts.
- **Shops bring buyers.** More shops make the brand easier to find. Doubling your shops brings about half as many buyers again, at the same price and marketing.
- **Changing your shops.** You can open or close shops each year, and change the staff in each. Finance knows what shops and staff cost.

These figures hold while your brand's share of the market is small; as it grows, each step brings a little less.
