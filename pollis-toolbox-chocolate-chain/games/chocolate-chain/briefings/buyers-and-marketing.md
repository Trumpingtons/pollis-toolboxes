# Buyers and Marketing

- **The market.** The country's buyers would buy about 1.5 million tablets in the first year. The market grows by about 4% a year, with surprises of several per cent either way.
- **How buyers choose.** Buyers compare every brand, and some buy none. What draws them is the beans, the price, the brand and the shops, and a brand's share depends on how it compares with all the others.
- **Price.** A price 10% higher loses about 16% of your buyers.
- **Marketing builds the brand.** Each year your brand is half of last year's brand plus this year's marketing, in thousand €: spending 50 every year builds a brand of 100. Going from no brand to 100 brings about 41% more buyers, and from 100 to 200 about 22% more: each thousand brings less than the one before.

These figures hold while your brand's share of the market is small; as it grows, each step brings a little less.
