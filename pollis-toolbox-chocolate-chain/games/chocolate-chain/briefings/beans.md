# Beans

- **What each bean costs**, for one tablet: Bulk Forastero 0.60 €, Fine Trinitario 1.00 €, Single-Origin Criollo 1.70 €.
- **What buyers think of them.** At the same price, Fine Trinitario draws about 1.8 times the buyers of Bulk Forastero, and Single-Origin Criollo about 3 times.
- **Put another way:** Fine Trinitario lets a brand charge between about 35% and 45% more than Bulk Forastero for the same buyers, and Single-Origin Criollo between about 73% and 99% more, as much as the buyers' sensitivity to price allows.

These figures hold while your brand's share of the market is small; as it grows, each step brings a little less.
