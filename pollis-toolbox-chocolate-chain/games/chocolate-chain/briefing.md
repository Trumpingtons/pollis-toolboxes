# Grow a Chocolate Chain

Your team runs a new chocolate brand in one country. You make every tablet you sell, and you sell them only in your own shops. Rival teams run the other brands, and the same buyers choose between all of you.

## The Game

- The game lasts five years. Each year your team sets its price, beans, shops, staff per shop and marketing, and the facilitator plays the year once every team has submitted.
- You start with no shops and 1,000,000 € of cash.
- After each year everyone sees every brand's price, beans, shops, market share, sales and profit. Only your team sees its own accounts: demand, capacity, lost sales, costs and cash.
- The team with the highest cumulative profit after five years wins.
- Your team can write to the other teams, beside the chat. The facilitator reads those messages too, and nothing makes a team keep what it promised.
- During the game the facilitator releases news, which can change the market or your costs from that year on. Some news reaches only some roles.

## Your Briefing

Your role's briefing, below this one, says what your team knows about the market and its costs. If your team has several roles, each knows something the others do not: talk before anyone submits.
